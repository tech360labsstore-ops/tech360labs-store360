Returns & Refunds Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

At Tech360Labs, we want every customer to be happy with their purchase. If something goes wrong with your order, contact us so we can review the issue and help.

1. Damaged or defective products

If your product arrives damaged or defective, contact us within 30 days of delivery. Include your order number, a description of the problem, and clear photos or a video showing the damage or defect. We will review the information and work with our supplier to determine whether a refund or replacement is available.

2. Incorrect or missing products

If you receive the wrong item or something is missing, contact us within 30 days of delivery. Please provide your order number and photos of what you received. We will review the issue and work toward an appropriate resolution.

3. Shipping delays and missing packages

If your order is delayed or appears to be missing, contact us with your order number. We will review the tracking information and investigate the shipment. Refunds or replacements for delayed or missing packages depend on the shipping investigation and applicable supplier policies. Tracking may show delivery even when a package needs to be collected from a local carrier or post office.

4. Change-of-mind returns

We do not currently guarantee returns for products simply because a customer changes their mind or no longer wants the item. Contact us before sending anything back. Unauthorized returns may not be accepted.

5. Return instructions and shipping costs

Do not send products back without contacting us first. If a return is approved, we will provide instructions. International returns may be expensive, and return shipping costs may be the customer's responsibility unless otherwise required by law. Do not mail a return to the supplier unless we provide the correct return instructions.

6. Contact us

For help with an order or a return request, email us. Please include your order number so we can assist you faster.
