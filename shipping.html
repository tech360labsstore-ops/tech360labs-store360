Shipping Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Thanks for shopping with Tech360Labs. This page explains what to expect about delivery and tracking.

Current earbuds estimate: The supplier estimate previously provided for the KP-227 wireless earbuds was 3–7 days for delivery within the United States. This is an estimate, not a guarantee, and must be reconfirmed with the supplier before an order is accepted. Shipping times and costs can change.

1. Processing and delivery estimates

Processing and transit times may vary depending on product availability, warehouse processing, shipping carrier, destination, and customs or other delivery conditions. We do not guarantee a specific delivery date.

Delivery estimates for other products are not yet confirmed. We will provide accurate product-specific shipping information before accepting orders for those items.

2. Shipping costs

Shipping costs can vary by product, destination, and shipping method. Any applicable shipping charge will be shown or confirmed before an order is placed. We will not describe shipping as free unless that is clearly stated for the specific offer.

3. Tracking

When tracking is available for the selected shipping method, we will share the tracking information after it is provided to us. Some shipping methods may have limited tracking, so tracking updates may not appear at every stage of delivery.

4. Delays

Carrier delays, weather, customs checks, incorrect or incomplete addresses, and other events outside our control can affect delivery. If your order appears delayed, contact us and we will review the available tracking information.

5. Please check your shipping address

Please enter a complete and accurate delivery address, including apartment or unit number where applicable. Contact us as soon as possible if you notice an error. We cannot guarantee that an address can be changed after an order has been processed or shipped.

6. Missing or delivered packages

If tracking says delivered but you cannot locate your package, please check around your delivery location and with household members or your local carrier first, then contact us. We will review the information available and explain the next steps.

7. Contact us

For shipping questions, email tech360labs.store@gmail.com. Include your order number so we can help.
