Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Tech360Labs respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may be handled when you visit our website or contact us. We will update this page if the way the store operates changes.

1. Information you provide

The current storefront does not provide customer accounts or an order-submission form. If you email us, we receive the information you include, such as your email address, name, order details, and the contents of your message. Please do not send payment-card numbers or passwords by email.

2. Shopping bag and website storage

The shopping bag on this website is a demonstration feature. It stores selected item quantities in your browser's local storage so the bag can work while you use the site. This information stays in that browser unless you clear its site data. The shopping bag is not currently connected to an order-processing system.

3. Payments

The separate earbuds checkout link currently uses Stripe test mode and is for testing only; it does not accept real customer payments. Do not enter real payment details into a test checkout. If Tech360Labs later enables live payments, the payment provider's privacy policy will also apply to information it collects and processes. We will update this policy to describe the live checkout before accepting orders.

4. Suppliers and service providers

The website is hosted by Netlify. If the store begins accepting orders and we use a dropshipping supplier to fulfill them, we may need to share the customer's name, delivery address, contact details, and order information with that supplier and shipping providers so the order can be fulfilled. The current storefront does not automatically send orders to CJdropshipping.

5. Website hosting and technical information

Our hosting provider may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, and server request data as part of operating and protecting the website. For details about hosting-provider data practices, review Netlify's privacy information.

6. How we use information

Information you send us may be used to reply to your questions, investigate an order issue if order processing is enabled, provide customer support, and protect the store against misuse. We do not sell personal information.

7. Retention and security

We keep messages and any order-related information only as long as reasonably needed for support, business records, legal obligations, or resolving disputes. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

You may contact us to ask about personal information you have sent us or request correction or deletion where applicable. Some information may need to be retained to meet legal or recordkeeping obligations. Privacy rights vary by location.

9. Children's privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the store's features and practices change. The updated date at the top of this page will show when changes are made.

11. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, email tech360labs.store@gmail.com.
